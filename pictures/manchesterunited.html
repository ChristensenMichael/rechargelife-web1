Back to Manchester United FC’s training center for the annual maintenance of the MEDITERRANEAN hyperbaric chamber (4-seater).

With this occasion, we also moved the chamber to a more accessible part of the complex, which meant we had to take everything apart and build it back together in the second location.

Good thing the design is modular, it makes assembly so much easier.

The MEDITERRANEAN chamber is perfect for sports recovery centers given its generous capacity to accommodate 4 athletes and provide visibility through the 2 large windows.

With ASME PVHO certification, the hyperbaric chamber (2.0 ATA) is built for very effective hyperbaric oxygen therapy sessions, with streamlined oxygen through the BIBS mask and a comfortable custom interior for 4 people to enjoy the therapy.
